Yes, free restaurant billing software for Android exists, and you can run billing on a phone without paying. Free apps can print GST bills on a Bluetooth printer and keep a simple sales record. What they usually don't do well is tables, KOTs to the kitchen, several staff phones, and reports you can trust. That is how they get you to upgrade.

Here is what to expect from free billing apps, the limits to check before you depend on one, and when paying makes sense.

1. Kinds of free restaurant billing software for Android

General billing apps (for example Khatabook, myBillBook, Vyapar) are made for all kinds of shops. They make GST invoices well and have free or very cheap plans, but have no real table or KOT handling.

Free POS apps (for example Loyverse) are made for cafes and shops. They handle items, modifiers and kitchen printing, but are less tuned to Indian restaurant habits like running table bills and Indian GST bill formats.

Free tiers of restaurant software: some restaurant billing apps have a free plan or a long free trial. Check whether "free" means free forever or only for 7–30 days.

Look at the app's Google Play rating, the last update date and recent reviews before you install. An app not updated in a year is a risk.

2. What to check before relying on a free app

  • GST bill format: GSTIN, invoice number series, CGST/SGST split and your FSSAI number on the bill.
  • Printer support: does it print to your Bluetooth or USB thermal printer?
  • Offline billing: can you keep billing when the internet goes, and does it sync later?
  • Tables and KOT: can you keep a running bill per table and send items to the kitchen?
  • Limits: number of bills, items, devices or users on the free plan.
  • Data: can you export your sales to Excel? Is it backed up if the phone is lost?
  • Ads: some free apps show ads, or print their own branding on your bills.

3. Offline billing: what it really means

"Offline" can mean two different things. Some apps store everything on the phone and never need the internet (but your data is lost if the phone is lost). Others work online but keep billing during an outage and sync later. The second kind is safer. Test it: turn on airplane mode, make three bills, turn it off, and check that they appear in reports.

4. When free is enough

  • A food cart, tea stall or kiosk with one person billing.
  • A small takeaway counter without table service.
  • A new cafe testing the business for the first few months.

5. When it's time to pay

  • You have tables and need running bills and KOTs to the kitchen.
  • More than one person bills or takes orders.
  • You need to know daily sales, cash vs UPI, and best-selling items without counting by hand.
  • You want online orders (QR table ordering, your own website, WhatsApp) to reach the kitchen automatically.

FAQ

Is there a completely free restaurant billing app?

Yes, some apps have free plans, but they usually limit features, devices or bills, or show ads. Read the free plan's limits before you depend on it.

Can I print bills from my phone?

Yes. Most billing apps print to a small Bluetooth thermal printer costing ₹2,000–₹4,000.

Can I make GST bills with a free app?

Many free apps can, but check that the bill shows your GSTIN, invoice number, CGST/SGST split and FSSAI number.

What happens to my data if I lose my phone?

If the app stores data only on the phone, it may be lost. Choose an app with cloud backup.

Summary

Free Android billing apps are fine for carts, kiosks and small counters. Check GST format, printer support, offline billing and limits first. Once you have tables, a kitchen and several staff, a proper restaurant system saves more than it costs.
