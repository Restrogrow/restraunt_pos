Petpooja alternatives are worth a look when your restaurant's needs change, not because one tool is "bad". Petpooja is a well-known restaurant POS in India, with offline billing, station-wise KOT printing and Swiggy/Zomato order integration listed on its own website. Many owners stay happy with it. Others compare options when they open a bar, start a chain, or want more direct orders.

This guide explains common reasons owners compare, what to check before switching any POS, and other options with what each company says it focuses on. We have not listed prices or ratings: ask each vendor for a written quote.

1. Why restaurants look at Petpooja alternatives

These are the usual reasons any restaurant compares POS software. They are not claims about Petpooja itself:

  • The business changed: a second outlet, a bar counter, a cloud kitchen brand or a franchise.
  • Total cost: the yearly cost with add-ons, extra devices and outlets may look different from the first quote.
  • A different focus: some owners want more help with their own online orders, loyalty and WhatsApp, others want deeper chain controls.
  • Hotel needs: restaurants inside hotels may want bills to go to the guest's room folio.
  • Support style: some owners prefer a smaller team they can reach directly, others want a large support centre.

2. What to check before you switch

Your data

Ask your current provider how to download your menu, past bills, customer list and reports, and keep a copy before you cancel. Ask the new provider how they will load your menu, so you don't re-type hundreds of items.

KOT, BOT and printers

Test your own billing printer, kitchen printer and bar printer with the new software. Check that food goes on the KOT and drinks on a BOT if you have a bar, and that each station gets only its own items. Our KOT printer troubleshooting guide helps if a printer won't connect.

Swiggy and Zomato

If many orders come from delivery apps, check whether the new POS receives aggregator orders directly or whether staff will need to enter them by hand. Ask how menu and stock changes reach the apps.

Offline billing

Turn off the Wi-Fi during the trial and bill a few orders. Bills should continue and sync when the internet is back.

Support and training

Ask who sets up your menu, who trains your staff, and how to reach support on a Saturday night. Call once during the trial to see.

The switch-over day

Pick a slow day, run both systems in parallel for a day or two, and only then stop the old one. Keep the old login until your accountant has the last month's reports.

FAQ

What are the main Petpooja alternatives in India?

Restaurants often compare Restroworks (Posist), GoFrugal, DotPe and Rista, eZee Optimus and RestroGrow. Each focuses on something different: chains, aggregator orders, QR payments, hotels or direct orders and loyalty.

Will I lose my sales history if I switch POS?

Not if you download your reports and bills before you cancel. Old history usually stays in your old account or your export; the new POS starts fresh from the switch date.

Can I keep my existing printers?

Usually yes. Most thermal billing and KOT printers work with any POS that supports USB, network or Bluetooth printing. Test them during the trial.

How long does a switch take?

For one outlet, a few days: menu, tables, printers, staff logins, then a day or two running both systems.

Summary

Look at Petpooja alternatives when your restaurant's needs change, not on a whim. Before switching any POS, save your data, test your printers, check aggregator orders and offline billing, and run both systems for a day. Then pick the option whose focus matches your restaurant: chains, delivery, hotels, or direct orders and loyalty.
