2. What the Swastik software price in Nepal usually covers, and what it does not

When the swastik software price in Nepal is quoted as a single figure, ask what is inside it:

  • How many computers? Licences are commonly per machine. A counter plus a manager's laptop is two.
  • Is the restaurant or hotel module included, or is the base price for trading and the table-service part extra?
  • Yearly support and updates: usually a percentage of the licence, every year. Tax rules change; an unsupported copy ages badly.
  • Installation and training: billed separately more often than not.
  • Printer and hardware: almost never included. Budget for it — KOT and bill printer prices in Nepal has the bands.
  • Data migration: if you are moving from something else, ask who does it and what it costs.

3. The six questions that matter more than the price

  1. Does it print a correct Nepali bill by itself? Service charge before VAT, 13% VAT on its own line, your PAN on the bill, bill numbers running in order inside the fiscal year, Bikram Sambat dates. If any of that needs a workaround, the workaround becomes your problem every evening.
  2. Does it send KOTs to the kitchen and BOTs to the bar without someone walking the slip over?
  3. What happens when the internet drops? Desktop software keeps working and your accountant's data is on one machine. Cloud software needs a plan for outages. Ask which, and ask where the backup lives.
  4. Who fixes it at 9pm on a Saturday? A local dealer who answers the phone is worth more than a feature list.
  5. If you grow past the e-billing threshold, is this on the IRD's list and will the dealer handle the permission process? See IRD approved billing software in Nepal.
  6. Can you get your own data out? Ask for an export before you buy, not after you want to leave.

4. One-time licence or monthly subscription?

A one-time licence looks cheaper after three years and costs more in year one, which is exactly when a new restaurant is short of cash. A subscription costs more over time and lets you stop. Neither is wrong; what matters is which risk you would rather carry. If you are opening now, keep the first-year outlay small — the full picture is in the cost to open a restaurant in Nepal.

One honest warning about "free" copies: pirated installs of Nepali accounting software are common and they are a bad idea in a business that is audited. No updates when the tax rules change, no support when it breaks, and a tax office conversation you do not want.

Common questions

Is Swastik good for a restaurant? For accounts and VAT returns, it is well established and widely supported. For table service and kitchen tickets, check the restaurant module carefully against the six questions above before you decide.

What does IMS software cost in Nepal? Also quoted as a licence plus yearly support, in similar bands, and also varying by module and dealer. Get both quotes itemised and compare like with like.

Can I run accounting software and a POS together? Yes, and many do. Agree with your accountant how sales move from one to the other — a daily summary is usually enough.

Do I need any of this for a small momo shop? You need to bill every sale and keep the record. A phone and a thermal printer can do that. The question is not how much software, it is whether every plate gets billed.
