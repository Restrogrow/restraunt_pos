Zero commission food ordering means your customers order from your own website, app or WhatsApp instead of a delivery app, so nobody takes a percentage of the bill. You pay a fixed fee for your ordering software and the payment gateway's charge on online payments, and keep the rest. For regular customers, this is usually the single biggest profit improvement a restaurant can make.

2. Why customers stay on the apps (and how to answer each)

  • Habit: they open the app without thinking. Answer: put your ordering link and QR in every parcel.
  • Discounts: the app shows offers. Answer: a direct-only reward that beats them, like a free dessert or loyalty points.
  • Trust in payment: answer with UPI and cash on delivery on your site.
  • Order tracking: answer with order updates on WhatsApp or a tracking page.

4. Mistakes to avoid

  • Leaving the apps completely. They still bring new customers. Use them to find people, and your own channel to keep them.
  • Breaking app rules. Read your contract before putting offers in app parcels; keep inserts about your brand and your direct-order reward.
  • A slow or clumsy website. If ordering takes longer than on the app, people go back.
  • No reason to switch. "Order direct" alone isn't enough; give a clear reward.
  • Spamming customers. Two or three useful messages a month is plenty.

FAQ

Is zero commission ordering really free?

There is no commission per order. You still pay for your ordering software and the payment gateway fee on online payments, and you arrange delivery.

Will customers really order directly?

Regular customers often do, if ordering is quick and there is a clear reward. New customers usually still find you through the apps.

How do I deliver direct orders?

Use your own riders for nearby orders and a delivery partner service for longer distances.

Can I take zero commission orders on WhatsApp?

Yes. Share your ordering link in WhatsApp, or take orders in chat. See how to take orders on WhatsApp.
