The kitchen display system price in India runs from about ₹6,000 to over ₹35,000 per screen for hardware, plus ₹0 to about ₹1,800 a month for software. The cheapest working setup is an Android tablet on the kitchen wall running KDS software that comes with your billing system. Rugged, purpose-built kitchen screens cost more but survive heat and oil better.

1. How a kitchen display system works

When a waiter, the counter or an online order adds items, they appear on the kitchen screen instantly instead of printing as a KOT. Each order shows the table or order type, the items, notes, and a timer. The cook taps an order when it's being prepared and again when it's ready, and the waiter or counter is told it's ready to serve.

3. Software prices

  • Included with your POS: many restaurant systems include a kitchen screen at no extra cost. RestroGrow includes it in the staff app (chef role).
  • Separate KDS apps: from free basic versions to about ₹300–₹1,800 a month per outlet.
  • Enterprise systems: priced per screen and per outlet, usually on request.

A KDS must connect to your billing software. A stand-alone screen that doesn't receive your orders is useless, so choose the KDS that works with your POS.

5. When a KDS is worth it

  • You get many orders at once from several sources (dine-in, parcel, Swiggy, Zomato, your website).
  • Orders get lost or cooked twice with paper slips.
  • You run a cloud kitchen where speed per order matters.
  • You want to know how long dishes actually take to prepare.

For a small cafe with one cook and a few orders an hour, a printer is still fine.

6. Tips for setting up a kitchen screen

  1. Mount it at eye level, away from steam, oil and the sink.
  2. Use a tablet with a big, bright screen and keep it plugged in.
  3. Turn on sound alerts for new orders.
  4. Keep one printer as a backup for power or Wi-Fi problems.
  5. Show different stations (tandoor, Chinese, main) only their own items.

FAQ

What is the cost of a kitchen display system in India?

From about ₹6,000 for an Android tablet to ₹35,000+ for a rugged kitchen screen, plus software from free to about ₹1,800 a month.

Can I use a normal tablet as a kitchen display?

Yes, if your billing software has a kitchen display app. Use a wall mount and a case to protect it.

Does a KDS replace the KOT printer?

It can. Many restaurants still keep one printer as a backup or for the bar.

Does a KDS need internet?

It needs a connection to your billing system, usually over the restaurant's Wi-Fi. A stable router matters more than fast internet.

Summary

A kitchen display doesn't have to be expensive: a ₹6,000–₹10,000 Android tablet with KDS software included in your billing system is enough for most restaurants. Pay for rugged screens only in heavy-use kitchens, and keep a printer as backup.
