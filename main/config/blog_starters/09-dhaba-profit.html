Dhaba business profit per month depends on sales: a well-run dhaba in India usually keeps 10% to 20% of its sales as net profit. So a dhaba selling ₹3 lakh a month might keep ₹30,000 to ₹60,000, and a busy highway dhaba selling ₹10 lakh a month might keep ₹1 lakh to ₹2 lakh. Claims of 30–40% margins online are rare in real life, especially once rent and staff are counted.

Below are two worked examples, the setup cost, and the things that decide whether a dhaba makes money.

3. How much it costs to start

  • Small dhaba in a rented space: roughly ₹2 lakh to ₹8 lakh (kitchen equipment, tandoor, furniture, first month's stock, deposit).
  • Highway dhaba with land on lease: ₹10 lakh to ₹30 lakh+, depending on construction, parking and seating.
  • Licences: FSSAI registration or licence (apply on the FoSCoS portal), GST registration if your turnover crosses the limit, trade licence from the local body, and fire safety approval where required.

4. What decides a dhaba's profit

Location and footfall: the biggest factor. A highway spot near a toll plaza or a town market beats great food in a hidden lane.

Food cost: keep raw material at 30–38% of sales. Waste, over-portioning and staff meals quietly push it to 45%.

Menu size: a short menu of fast-moving dishes (dal, paneer, chicken, rotis, rice) wastes less than a 100-item menu.

Cash control: unbilled orders and cash leakage are common in dhabas. Bill every order, even a single chai.

Long hours, steady staff: high staff turnover hurts taste and service.

5. How to increase dhaba profit

  1. Bill everything through a system, so daily sales, cash and UPI always match.
  2. Track your top 10 dishes and their food cost; raise prices or portions where margin is thin.
  3. Add parcels and takeaway, which use no extra seating.
  4. Get on Google Maps with photos and reviews; highway travellers choose dhabas on Maps.

FAQ

Is a dhaba a profitable business in India?

Yes, with good location and tight cost control. Expect a 10–20% net margin; much higher numbers are unusual.

How much does a highway dhaba earn per month?

Busy highway dhabas can sell ₹5–15 lakh a month and keep around 10–20% as profit, depending on rent, staff and food cost.

How much investment is needed for a dhaba?

A small dhaba can start with ₹2–8 lakh; a highway dhaba with construction can need ₹10–30 lakh or more.

Which licence is needed for a dhaba?

FSSAI registration or licence is a must. Depending on your state and size, you may also need GST registration, a trade licence and fire safety approval.

Summary

A dhaba is a solid business when the location brings steady footfall and costs are watched daily. Plan on a 10–20% margin, keep food cost under 38%, keep the menu short, and bill every single order. Those habits matter more than any single recipe.
