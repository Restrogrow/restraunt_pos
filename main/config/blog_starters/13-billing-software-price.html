Restaurant billing software in India costs anywhere from ₹0 to ₹40,000+ a year per outlet. Simple restaurant apps start at about ₹300 a month, full POS systems with KOT, inventory and reports are usually ₹6,000 to ₹40,000 a year, and all-in-one systems that add your own ordering website and marketing cost ₹2,000 to ₹10,000 a month. What you actually pay in the first year is higher once you add GST, hardware and add-ons.

This guide explains how vendors price their software and how to work out the real total before you sign.

4. Is it worth it? Compare with commission

Most restaurants judge software only by its price. A better test is what it earns back. If you pay Swiggy and Zomato ₹20,000 a month in commission and the software moves even 20% of those orders to your own ordering website, it saves about ₹4,000 a month. See what Zomato commission really costs.

5. Questions to ask every vendor

  1. Is the price per outlet, per device or per user?
  2. Is GST included in the price shown?
  3. Which features are add-ons (inventory, aggregator integration, loyalty, extra devices)?
  4. Do I need your hardware, or can I use my own phone, tablet and printers?
  5. Does billing work offline? (See offline billing.)
  6. Are setup, menu upload and training free?
  7. Can I export my data if I leave?
  8. What is the refund rule on a yearly plan?

FAQ

How much does restaurant billing software cost per month in India?

About ₹300 to ₹1,000 a month for basic restaurant apps, and roughly ₹500 to ₹3,500 a month for a full POS when the yearly price is spread out. All-in-one systems with ordering and marketing cost ₹2,000 to ₹10,000 a month.

Is yearly billing cheaper than monthly?

Usually yes, by about 10–20%. Pay monthly for the first few months until you are sure the software fits, then switch to yearly.

Do I need to buy POS hardware?

Usually not. Most modern billing software runs on an Android phone, tablet or laptop with a thermal printer.

Is GST charged on restaurant software?

Yes, software subscriptions carry 18% GST. If your restaurant is GST-registered, ask your accountant whether you can claim it as input tax credit.

Summary

Restaurant billing software costs ₹0 to ₹40,000+ a year, but compare the first-year total, not the headline price: add GST, printers, add-ons and paper. Then ask what the software earns back, from faster billing to direct orders that skip commission.
