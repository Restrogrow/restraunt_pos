## Long description
RestroGrow helps restaurants, cafes and cloud kitchens grow direct orders and cut delivery app commissions.

Most restaurants pay 15–35% commission to third-party delivery apps on every order, even when the order comes from a loyal repeat customer. RestroGrow moves those regulars to the restaurant's own branded ordering system, where the commission is 0%. You keep selling on delivery apps, but you stop depending on them.

**Key features**
- **Direct ordering website:** your own branded online store with online payments and 0% commission.
- **WhatsApp CRM marketing:** save every customer's number, send offers and seasonal deals, and collect feedback.
- **Loyalty program:** customers earn points on direct orders and keep coming back.
- **QR menu:** scan-and-order at the table, which also captures the guest's contact.
- **Combos & upsells:** increase average order value at checkout.
- **Google Business Profile:** AI-assisted posts and review replies.
- **POS & inventory:** billing, stock alerts and sales reports.
- **Mobile app:** live orders, customer list and campaigns on your phone.
- **Multi-outlet:** manage every location from one dashboard.

**Done-for-you setup:** parcel inserts, table QR codes, WhatsApp offers and first-order rewards that move your customers to direct ordering. Deliver with your own riders or a delivery partner.

No. Orders placed on your own RestroGrow ordering website have 0% commission. You pay a simple subscription, not a cut of every order.

**Restaurant Management:** RestroGrow is AI-powered restaurant management software that runs your whole restaurant from one dashboard. Manage menus, tables, reservations, staff, orders and payments, see live sales and reports, and control every outlet in one place. AI tools help with Google reviews, posts and daily insights, so owners spend less time on admin and more on guests.

**Customer Loyalty Program:** RestroGrow's loyalty program rewards guests with points on every direct order, so they come back more often. Set your own earn and redeem rules, send birthday and win-back rewards, and run VIP tiers, scratch cards and challenges. Every guest's visits, spend and points are tracked automatically, so you know who your best customers are.

**Online Food Ordering:** Get your own branded online ordering website with RestroGrow, with 0% commission on every order. Customers order delivery, takeaway or dine-in, pay online, apply coupons and track their order live. Combos and upsells raise order value, and every order lands straight in your dashboard and mobile app, so you keep full control of your customers and your margins.

**Restaurant POS:** RestroGrow POS makes billing fast for dine-in, takeaway and delivery. Take orders by table, send tickets to the kitchen and accept multiple payment methods. Inventory updates as you sell, with low-stock alerts, and daily sales, item and staff reports show exactly how each outlet is doing. Online and POS orders all live in one system.

**WhatsApp Marketing:** RestroGrow turns WhatsApp into your restaurant's marketing channel. It saves every customer's number from online orders and QR menus, then lets you send offers, seasonal deals and new menu updates to the right segments. Automated messages handle order updates, feedback requests and win-back offers, and AI helps answer customer chats quickly.
