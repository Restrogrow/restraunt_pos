# RestroGrow blog: keyword plan

Researched 17 Oct 2026. Use it with Super Admin › Blog (Keywords tab and the AI draft button).

## How this was researched (and its limits)

1. **Real demand:** 1,560 searches were run against Google India's autocomplete (the suggestions people see while typing), for 60 restaurant-owner topics. That gave 6,020 real search phrases. A phrase that Google suggests for many different starting words gets searched a lot; its count is shown as "signal" below.
2. **How hard it is to rank:** for about 30 candidates, I looked at who ranks today. "Easy" means the top results are job ads, generic pages, marketplace listings or small blogs, with no proper answer. "Hard" means Petpooja's blog, Tally, ClearTax or news sites already answer it well.
3. **Limits:** this gives no exact monthly search numbers; that needs Google Keyword Planner or a paid tool like Ahrefs. The ranking checks used a US-based search engine with "India" added, so treat difficulty as a close guide, not exact Google India results. After a month, Search Console › Performance shows the real numbers for your own pages; use those to adjust.

## What we learned about the competition

- **Petpooja's blog** (blog.petpooja.com) owns the broad growth topics: loyalty programs, WhatsApp marketing, food cost, GST bill formats. Don't fight it there yet.
- **Tally, ClearTax, Taxmann** own GST tax questions.
- **DineOpen** (a small competitor) publishes a page on nearly every topic, in English and Hindi, but its pages are thin and self-promoting. It is beatable with better, more honest pages.
- **Nobody** has written proper pages on KOT basics, KOT printers, Swiggy fees, or taking orders without aggregators. These are RestroGrow's openings, and they match what RestroGrow sells.

## Rules for every post (what makes these rank)

1. **Answer in the first 2 lines.** For example: "KOT stands for Kitchen Order Ticket. It is the slip that tells the kitchen what to cook."
2. **Add proof only RestroGrow has:** screenshots of the real app, real prices in ₹, photos of actual printers and KOTs, and examples from your restaurants. AI drafts can't fake this, and it's what Google rewards.
3. **Keyword in:** the title, the web address, the first paragraph, one heading, and the cover's alt text. The editor's SEO checklist checks these.
4. **Be honest about competitors.** "Cheapest" and "alternative" posts should list real options fairly, with RestroGrow as one of them. Pages that only push their own product rank worse and convince nobody.
5. **Link:** each post to its group's pillar, and to 1–2 RestroGrow feature pages.
6. **Keep fee and price posts current.** Change the year in the title and the numbers every 3 months. Google favours fresh pages for "2026"-style searches.
7. **Cover at least 1200px wide**, with an FAQ section of 3–4 real questions at the end.

## Other things that will help ranking

- **Free tools rank well and earn links:** a Zomato/Swiggy commission calculator, a food cost calculator and a GST bill calculator. Each can be its own page. Claude can build these as pages on restrogrow.com when you ask.
- **Hindi posts later:** autocomplete shows Hindi searches like "cloud kitchen business kya hota hai", and only DineOpen is writing in Hindi. Once the 10 English posts are live, translate the best 5.
- **YouTube:** a 2–3 minute video per post (for example, fixing a KOT printer), embedded in the post.
- **Links from your restaurants' sites:** a "Powered by RestroGrow" link on each ordering site (on the to-do list).
