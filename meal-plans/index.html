How it works

Set it up once. It runs every day.

Make your plans

Lunch, dinner or both. Number of meals, price, bonus meals and a photo. As many plans as you like.

Set the weekly menu

What's in the tiffin each day, Monday to Sunday, with dish photos. Days without a menu are days off.

Customers subscribe

On your website they pick a plan, start date and address, then pay by UPI, QR or cash.

Meals go to the kitchen

Every day each customer's meal becomes an order with a kitchen ticket, and one meal is counted off.

Everyone in control

Your customer handles the small things. You see everything.

Your customer can

  • Skip any day — going out or travelling? The meal stays in their plan for later.
  • Pause and resume — for a holiday, without losing a single meal.
  • Change address and phone — moving to the office? Today's waiting meal follows the new address.
  • See meals left and the next delivery — no "how many are left?" calls.
  • Track every delivery — received, preparing, delivered.
  • Renew in one tap — when the plan ends.

You can

  • See every subscriber — meals left, next delivery, payment, skips and address.
  • Do it for them — skip a day, pause or change the address when a customer calls.
  • Give meals back — for a late or missed delivery, in one click.
  • Mark cash and QR payments received — and see who still has to pay.
  • Sign up walk-in customers — from the counter, with or without an account.
  • Owner and manager only — staff can't give away meals or end a plan.

Questions

Good to know

Do my customers need to download an app?

No. They subscribe and manage their plan on your restaurant's website, from any phone.

How are meals counted?

One meal is counted off for each delivery day. A skipped day, a paused day or a day without a menu doesn't count. When meals run out, the plan ends and the customer can renew.

Can a customer skip today?

Yes, until today's meal goes to the kitchen. After that the day can't be skipped, so your kitchen never cooks for nothing.

How do customers pay?

Online by UPI or card, by scanning your QR code, or in cash. You can mark cash and QR payments as received from your dashboard.

Do I need to create orders every day?

No. Each morning the day's meals become orders on their own, with kitchen tickets, alongside your normal orders. There's also a button to send them yourself.

Can I offer refunds or end a plan?

Yes. The owner or a manager can end a plan, and no more meals are sent. Any refund is settled with the customer directly.
