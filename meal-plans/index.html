While you sleep

Every morning, the day's tiffins make themselves.

No list to write, no orders to punch. Each customer due today becomes a normal order with a kitchen ticket, next to your other orders.

  • Night
    Customers make their changesSkips, pauses and new addresses, right up to the morning.
  • Early
    Today's orders are createdSkipped, paused and no-menu days are left out on their own.
  • Then
    Kitchen tickets go outTo your kitchen screen or printer, with the day's menu, meal time and address. One meal is counted off.
  • Lunch
    Out for deliveryEach order carries the customer's latest address and phone.
  • Done
    Customer sees "Delivered"And how many meals are left. No message needed.

Do my customers need to download an app?

No. They subscribe and manage their plan on your restaurant's website, from any phone.

How are meals counted?

One meal is counted off for each delivery day. Skipped days, paused days and days without a menu don't count. When the meals run out, the plan ends and the customer can renew.

Can a customer skip today?

Yes, until today's meal goes to the kitchen. After that the day can't be skipped, so your kitchen never cooks for nothing.

How do customers pay?

Online by UPI or card, by scanning your QR code, or in cash. You can mark cash and QR payments as received from your dashboard, or switch cash off.

Do I need to create orders every day?

No. Each morning the day's meals become orders on their own, with kitchen tickets, next to your normal orders. There's also a button to send them yourself.

Can I end a plan or give a refund?

Yes. The owner or a manager can end a plan and no more meals are sent. Any refund is settled with the customer directly.
