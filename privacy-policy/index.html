Privacy Policy

What data RestroGrow collects from restaurants, their staff and their guests, why we need it, and how we keep it safe.

    1. Introduction

    Restro Grow ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our restaurant management system and services.

    2. Information We Collect

    We collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

    • Restaurant name, contact information, and business details
    • Staff member information (name, email, role)
    • Customer information (for order processing and reservations)
    • Payment information (processed securely through third-party payment processors)
    • Menu items, pricing, and inventory data
    • Order history and transaction records

    3. How We Use Your Information

    We use the information we collect to:

    • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
    • Process orders and manage restaurant operations
    • Send you service-related communications
    • Generate reports and analytics
    • Comply with legal obligations
    • Protect against fraud and unauthorized access

    4. Data Security

    We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

    5. Data Retention

    We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us.

    6. Your Rights

    You have the right to:

    • Access your personal information
    • Correct inaccurate data
    • Request deletion of your data
    • Object to processing of your data
    • Data portability

    7. Third-Party Services

    Our service may contain links to third-party websites or integrate with third-party payment processors. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties.

    8. Changes to This Policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

    9. Contact Us

    If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
