Why WhatsApp

Your customers are already on WhatsApp.

Many regulars already send their order to your number. Then someone has to read each message, check prices, type it into billing and reply, and on a busy night messages get missed. Our guide on how to take orders on WhatsApp covers the manual way.

Restro Grow's WhatsApp ordering does the reading and typing for you. The customer chats, the order is checked against your live menu, and it lands in the same place as your table QR orders and website orders, with no commission. See everything Restro Grow does.

Type it any way

Customers type the way they talk.

“Ek biryani aur 2 coke, kam spicy.” Restro Grow picks out the dishes, quantities and notes, matches them to your menu and shows the cart with the total.

  • English, Hindi or Hinglish, typed or tapped.
  • Buttons, lists and dish photos for customers who'd rather tap than type.
  • Asks for the size when a dish comes in half and full.
  • “Did you mean…?” when a word isn't on the menu, with a button to add the right dish.

Chat on WhatsApp
Do customers need to install anything?

No. They message your WhatsApp number like any other chat.

What do I need to start?

A Restro Grow account and a WhatsApp number connected through Repeat Grow, our WhatsApp CRM, in Growth › WhatsApp. We can help you connect it: call or WhatsApp +91 63775 68749.

Does it understand Hindi and Hinglish?

Yes. Customers can write in English, Hindi or Hinglish, like “do butter naan aur ek paneer tikka”. If a word doesn't match a dish, it asks “did you mean…?” instead of guessing.

Can customers pay online inside WhatsApp?

Not yet. WhatsApp orders are paid on delivery, at pickup or at the table. Customers who want to pay online can order on your ordering website instead.

What if the bot gets an order wrong?

Before anything is placed, the customer sees the full order with the total and taps Confirm or Change. Only dishes on your menu, at your menu prices, can be ordered.

Can I still reply to customers myself?

Yes. When a customer asks for a person, the bot goes quiet and the chat is marked for you in Customer chats, where you can reply. Typing “order” starts the bot again.

Does the order reach my kitchen?

Yes. It arrives with a new-order alert like a website order. Dine-in orders with a table go to the kitchen as a KOT straight away; takeaway and delivery orders go ahead once your staff accept them. The customer gets status updates on WhatsApp.
