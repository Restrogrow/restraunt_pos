Why we exist

A restaurant shouldn't need six tools and a notebook.

Walk into most restaurants and you'll find the same pile: a billing machine, a tablet for every delivery app, a printer for the kitchen, a WhatsApp group for regulars, a notebook for credit, and a spreadsheet nobody updates.

And on every order that comes through a delivery app, a cut goes out, and so does the customer. The app keeps their number. The restaurant keeps the bill.

We built Restro Grow to put all of it in one place the restaurant owns: one login for billing, kitchen and tables, a website where customers order directly, and the tools to bring those customers back.

Great food deserves great tools. And the customer who loved it should be yours to call again.
