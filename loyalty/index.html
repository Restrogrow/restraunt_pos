Why loyalty

Your best marketing is the customer who already came.

Winning a new customer costs ads and commissions. Bringing back someone who already liked your food costs a small gift.

01

Delivery apps keep the customer

On aggregators the customer's number belongs to the app. With Restro Grow every order with a phone number builds your own customer list.

02

Most first visits are also the last

A customer who isn't reminded forgets you. A gift on their birthday, or after a few quiet weeks, gives them a reason to return.

03

You don't have time to run it

No cards to print, no Excel, no messages to send by hand. You set the rules once — the gifts go out on their own.

Questions

Good to know

Do my customers need to download an app?

No. They earn with their phone number. They can check their points on your restaurant's website, and gifts arrive on WhatsApp.

Which orders count?

Orders at your counter (POS), QR orders at the table and orders on your website — any order with the customer's phone number.

Do I need WhatsApp to use loyalty?

No. Points, cashback, stamps, gifts and games all work without it. Connecting WhatsApp lets the gift messages and campaigns go out automatically.

Can I control how much I give away?

Yes. You set what each reward is worth, a minimum order, how long a gift is valid and the prize budget for games.

Can customers misuse gift codes?

Each gift code belongs to one customer's phone number and works once, so it can't be forwarded to a group.
