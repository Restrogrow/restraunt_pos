    Questions

    Owners usually ask

    How does the ₹500 booking work?

    Pay ₹500 online to book. We build your menu and send it on WhatsApp for approval. Pay the balance in cash or UPI when your standees are delivered.

    How fast will I get it?

    Your menu is live within 24 hours of receiving your menu. Standees are printed and shipped fast across India after you approve the design.

    Can someone order from home with a photo of the QR, or place fake orders?

    No order goes to your kitchen until your staff accepts it. Every order shows the table, the guest's name and phone number, so you always know who ordered. You can ask guests to pay by UPI before you accept, and block any number in one tap.

    What is the difference between QR Menu and QR Ordering?

    QR Menu (₹1,999) is a digital menu guests can scan and browse. QR Ordering (₹4,999) adds ordering and payment from the table, kitchen tickets, order alerts, customer data and offers, with 10 standees free.

    Can I order more standees?

    Yes. Add as many as you need for ₹99 each, on this page or at checkout.

    My customers are not tech-savvy. Will they use it?

    If they can open the phone camera, they can use it. No app, no login. Staff can still take orders the usual way.

    Where do I see the orders?

    On the RestroGrow dashboard and the RestroGrow Partner app, with a sound alert for every new order. KOT can print on your printer.

    Do you take commission on orders?

    No. 0% commission. Your orders and your customer data are yours.
