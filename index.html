Restaurant POS & billing software

A bill in a few taps, even with a queue at the counter.

  • Search dishes with “/”, filter veg, non-veg and egg, pick sizes and add-ons
  • Split one bill across cash, UPI and card, or collect later
  • Hold a bill and come back to it; every discount is capped and logged
  • Keeps billing when the internet drops, and syncs when it's back
  • Prints on thermal printers over Bluetooth, USB or the network

KOT management & kitchen display

The kitchen sees every order the second it's placed, from the counter, a table QR or the website.

  • Every order becomes a KOT with a running timer
  • Chef screen with New, Cooking and Ready; tickets turn amber at 10 minutes and red at 20
  • Waiters' phones ring when food is ready or a table calls
  • Print KOTs, or go fully paperless

Table management, QR menus & reservations

  • Online table booking that won't double-book a table

Questions

Before you sign up

More answers on the FAQ page, or ask in the chat at the bottom right.

Do I need to buy new hardware for Restro Grow?

No. Restro Grow runs in the browser on the Android tablet, iPad, Windows PC or phone you already have. Bills and KOTs print on thermal printers over Bluetooth, USB or the network.

What happens when the internet goes down?

The POS keeps billing offline and the bills sync by themselves when the internet is back. Loyalty points need the internet.

Do you take a commission on online orders?

No. Orders on your own Restro Grow ordering website are 0% commission. You pay only the flat monthly plan. If you take online payments, the payment gateway's fee is separate.

Can I keep using Swiggy and Zomato?

Yes. Many owners keep the apps for new customers and use QR codes, WhatsApp and loyalty in Restro Grow to bring regulars to their own website, where there is no commission.

How long is the free trial?

7 days, with no credit card and no setup fee. Most restaurants are set up in about 5 minutes.

Can I run more than one outlet?

Yes. The Multiple Locations plan gives owners a branch switcher to compare today's numbers per branch and open any branch from one login.
